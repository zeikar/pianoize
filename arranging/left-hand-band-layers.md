# The left hand, the band's layers

The left hand of a full arrangement, for listening, not for playing, taken from the band layer by layer. Take its parts as the layering rules in `right-hand-full.md` say: every part in its own register, what muddies the parts together left out, and one figure per layer, per section, recorded under "Figures" in `notes.md`. `arranging-rules.md` gives what every arrangement shares.

- **`L` takes the bass and the parts below about middle C.**
- **The bass line's motion.** `L`'s lowest note follows `electric_bass` where it walks, steps to a slash bass or pushes ahead of the bar line with the kick. A walk in quarter-note triplets is written at `8`, `10.667` and `13.333`, each `2.667` long.
- **The bass's figure** is one of the section's layers: held through the bar or struck short.
- **An open low register.** Below C3, no two notes closer than a fifth.
- **No limits.** The hand may hold any number of keys, stretch, leap and cross the right hand: under `--full` the check flags none of it, and this hand asks nothing more of the check.
